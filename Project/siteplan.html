Site Name

Dhalia's Bakery - a small bakery showcasing pastries, seasonal menus, and a newsletter signup. My wife is the one who makes the pastries and we decided to name it after a puppy that we had adopted a couple of years ago.

Domain option: dhaliasbakery.com

Site Purpose

Provide a visual meu of pastries, share store hours and location, accept newsletter signup and display seasonal promotions.

  • Showcase pastry photos and descriptions
  • Collect email and addresses for the newsletter
  • Provide hours, address, and contact info

Scenarios

  1. What are the different pastries that are offered -> There will be a photo album of the different pastries that are offered.
  2. How can I find out about other promotional discounts later on? -> There will be a way to sign up for a newsletter to receive emails for other promotions.

Color scheme

  • Primary Lilac: #D5B6FF — used for headings, accents, and decorative elements.
  • Brand Brown: #6A4226 — used for outlines, icons, borders, and emphasis.
  • Soft Beige Background: #FFF8EB — used for background sections and soft panels.
  • Neutral Text: #222222 — used for body text for readability.

Typography

Fonts chosen (2):

  1. Montserrat — headings and navigation (used in this document for headings).
  2. Tinos — body text, paragraphs (good legibility for long text).

Font Sizes: h1 2rem, h2 1.25rem, body 1rem (will just with responsive css).
